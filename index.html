Privacy Policy — Sponge Bands

Last updated: 2026-09-30

Sponge Bands ("the app") is an offline game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal or device information. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, or crash reporting, and it does not request Internet access.

Data stored on your device

Game progress and settings (for example completed levels, sound and vibration preferences) are saved only on your device using Android's local app storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

  • Vibrate – used only for haptic feedback during gameplay. You can turn it off in the game settings.
