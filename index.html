Privacy Policy — Sponge Bands

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Sponge Bands ("the app") is a puzzle game. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

Information we collect

The app has no accounts, no ads, and never asks for a name, email, photo, contact or location.

From version 1.0.3 the app sends anonymous gameplay statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase, so we can see which levels are too hard and improve the game. This is limited to:

  • gameplay events: a level was started or won (level number, stars, number of misses), a hint was used, a sticker was won;
  • basic technical information that Firebase collects automatically: device model, Android/iOS version, app version, language, country (not precise location), and an app-instance ID that is random and resets if the app is reinstalled.

The app also sends crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics when it crashes or freezes: the error details, device model, OS and app version, and a random installation ID, so we can fix the problem. No personal information is included.

The advertising ID and other device identifiers (such as the Android ID) are not collected. The data is not used for advertising or personalisation, and it is not sold or shared with anyone other than Google acting as our analytics provider. Data is sent encrypted. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data stored on your device

Game progress and settings (completed levels, coins, stickers, sound, vibration and reminder preferences) are saved only on your device. This data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

  • Internet – used only to send the anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports above. The game itself works fully offline.
  • Vibrate – haptic feedback during gameplay. Can be turned off in Settings.
  • Notifications – an optional daily "come back and play" reminder, created on the device itself (no server). Can be turned off in Settings or declined when asked.
  • Run at startup – only to restore that reminder after the phone restarts.

Children

The app is suitable for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Analytics is limited to the anonymous gameplay statistics described above, with advertising identifiers and ad personalisation switched off.
